SIMPLE STEPS. SHARED CLARITY.

How escrow works

Bring everyone into the same room before money or work changes hands.

01

Create or join

Open a room and choose buyer, seller or third-party role. Share the unique five-character code.

02

Agree together

Review the amount and delivery terms in one shared room. Messages and status updates stay with the deal.

03

Deposit & deliver

The buyer deposits using an available payment channel. The seller delivers after the deposit is verified.

04

Release or resolve

The buyer approves release when satisfied. If there is a problem, request help and an admin reviews the case.
