A NEUTRAL PLACE TO COMPLETE A DEAL

Keep the money, terms and next step in view.

XWADDA is a neutral middle layer for online transactions. Participants agree the terms in a shared room, follow payment and delivery milestones, and keep their messages and updates together. Where a supported payment channel is connected, funds are held for the deal until the agreed steps are completed. If something goes wrong, a participant can raise an issue in the room and the case follows the recorded agreement and transaction details.

A STRAIGHTFORWARD PROCESS

How XWADDA works

Start a room, invite the other people with a short code and follow each step in the same place.

01

Open a deal room

Add the title, amount and delivery terms. Choose whether you are the buyer, seller or third party.

02

Invite the other side

Share the unique five-character code. Each person signs in and claims an available role in the room.

03

Deposit and deliver

The buyer deposits the agreed amount. Once the deposit is confirmed, the seller can submit delivery and a note.

04

Review and finish

The buyer can approve the delivery and request release, or raise an issue for the deal to be reviewed.

PAYMENT OPTIONS

Choose the rail agreed for the deal

Available options include M-Pesa through HashBack HashPay and USDT on TRC20 or BEP20. The payment method and amount should be confirmed by the participants before sending funds.

  • M-Pesa collection using your HashPay checkout configuration
  • USDT deposits on the selected network with transaction reference
  • Fee shown alongside the deal amount before payment
  • Payment and payout status recorded in the shared timeline
Know the payment status

A submitted payment is not treated as received until the transaction is confirmed. Follow the instructions shown for the selected payment rail and keep your receipt or transaction hash.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you start

Who can join a room?

Signed-in accounts can join as buyer, seller or third party if that seat and display name are still available.

Can I cancel a deal?

A participant can cancel an unfunded room. Once a deposit is recorded, use the room’s issue or refund request so the deal can be reviewed.

How are release and refunds handled?

Release requests and refund requests are recorded against the room. Payment settlement and refunds are processed after the request is reviewed; check the room status and payment instructions for the current stage.

Does XWADDA show sample deals in my history?

No. The dashboard shows rooms linked to your signed-in account. The example room on this page is only an illustration.
