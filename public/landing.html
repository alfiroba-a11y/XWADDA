A NEUTRAL PLACE TO COMPLETE A DEAL

Keep the money, terms and next step in view.

XWADDA is a neutral middle layer for online transactions. Participants agree the terms in a shared room, follow payment and delivery milestones, and keep their messages and updates together. Where a supported payment channel is connected, funds are held for the deal until the agreed steps are completed. If something goes wrong, a participant can raise an issue in the room and the case follows the recorded agreement and transaction details.

FOUR STEPS FROM AGREEMENT TO PAYOUT

One room. A clear next step.

Every deal follows the same shared sequence, so both sides can see what comes next.

STEP 1

Agree the terms

Buyer and seller settle the item, price, delivery method and deadline before the buyer funds the deal. The agreement is recorded in the room.

STEP 2

Buyer pays into escrow

The buyer funds the agreed amount by a supported payment method. The room shows the deposit status and payment reference.

STEP 3

Seller delivers

Once the deposit is confirmed, the seller delivers the goods or service and records a delivery note for the buyer.

STEP 4

Release or dispute

The buyer reviews the delivery and requests release, or raises an issue in the room so the participants can resolve it using the recorded terms.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you start

Who can join a room?

Signed-in accounts can join as buyer, seller or third party if that seat and display name are still available.

Can I cancel a deal?

A participant can cancel an unfunded room. Once a deposit is recorded, use the room’s issue or refund request so the deal can be reviewed.

How are release and refunds handled?

Release requests and refund requests are recorded against the room. Payment settlement and refunds are processed after the request is reviewed; check the room status and payment instructions for the current stage.

Does the preview appear in my account history?

No. Only rooms linked to your signed-in account appear there. The calculator on this page is only a quote preview.
