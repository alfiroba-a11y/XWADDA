COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you start

How does escrow protect my deal?

The buyer’s payment is held while the seller completes the agreed delivery. Both parties can follow the deal and raise an issue if needed.

How do I pay?

Available payment options are shown when you create or join a deal. The USDT kiosk supports M-Pesa and selected crypto networks.

What if there is a problem?

Contact support from the deal and share the relevant details so the issue can be reviewed.
